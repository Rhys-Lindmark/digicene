# The Digicene is coming.

We’re building a new, hyperintelligent alien species on Earth, artificial intelligence. Soon, Earth’s brainpower will be dominated by the trillions of AI models rather than the billions of humans.

This is a big deal.

It will shape the Earth, just as humans have shaped the Earth during the last 200,000 years. We call the human age the Anthropocene. We’ll call the AI age the Digicene.

This book is an attempt to understand the Digicene, our newest age.

We could start in 2022 with the rise of ChatGPT. But that would only give us a surface-level understanding. To understand more deeply, we must travel further into the past.

We must walk the road of time, examining the objects we find along the way, and asking, “how did you come to be?”

Luckily, there is a process that creates it all—evolution. This process, the selection of inherited variation, applies to all populations, not just animal populations. Evolution is the best idea anyone has ever had. We will use it as a throughline.

In fact, we can go one step further. Order is governed not just by evolution, but by domestication. Let me explain. We often think of humans domesticating dogs and sheep. But we’ve also domesticated fire, water (irrigation), and soil (pottery).

Human domestication takes what’s lying around, tinkers with it, and then mass produces a new helpful object. This is the crystallization of repeated phenomena. Domestication.

This crystallization is a throughline through all time. Each level builds on the last. Atoms build life build sapiens build AI.

So we’ll use evolution and domestication to understand the road of time. This road has four parts: atoms, life, sapiens, and AI. With each, we’ll look at how it evolved and then understand its status today, at the start of the Digicene.
